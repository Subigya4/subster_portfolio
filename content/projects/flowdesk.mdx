## Overview

FlowDesk is a project management tool for small product teams: boards, timelines,
and lightweight docs in one workspace, designed to feel fast on day one and still
fast with ten thousand tasks.

## My role

Lead engineer — architecture, data model, the real-time collaboration layer and the
front-end component system.

## Highlights

- **Real-time boards** with optimistic updates and conflict-free merges, so two
  people can drag cards at once without clobbering each other.
- **Command palette** for keyboard-first navigation; most actions are two keystrokes away.
- **Sub-second search** across tasks, comments and docs using PostgreSQL full-text search.
- **Role-based access** with per-workspace permissions and audit log.

## Stack

Next.js (App Router), TypeScript, PostgreSQL with Prisma, WebSockets, Tailwind CSS,
deployed on Vercel with a managed Postgres instance.

## Outcome

Replaced three separate tools for the pilot customers and cut their weekly planning
meeting from an hour to twenty minutes.
